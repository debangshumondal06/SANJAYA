When to use SANJAYA vs National Emergency Services (112)

SANJAYA is designed for non-emergency civil infrastructure (damaged roads, waste bins, blocked drains, dark lights). If you encounter active structural collapse, gas leaks, sparking downed electric wires in flooded streets, or medical emergencies, do not wait for an online ticket. Call 112 (National Emergency Helpline) immediately.
