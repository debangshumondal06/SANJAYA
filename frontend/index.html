Clear Civic Journey

Three steps, one visible path

From the moment you notice a hazard to verified completion on the ground, every stage is transparent.

01

Report with Context

Describe what you see, attach a clear photograph or record a quick voice note, and pinpoint your street location. No login or password required.

02

Review & Department Routing

Our municipal coordinator confirms jurisdiction, checks against neighboring duplicates, and issues an official work order to the specialized field crew.

03

Follow Transparent Progress

Track your ticket anytime with plain-language updates, scheduled work windows, and verified photographic closure from the site.

Status Transparency

Know what happens next, in plain words

No obscure bureaucracy codes. SANJAYA translates internal engineering workflows into clear milestones so residents always know what to expect.

Capturing Useful Evidence

Step back two paces to capture both the defect and nearby surroundings. Good context helps dispatch choose the right repair truck.

Accurate Landmarks

Pair GPS coordinates with a distinct physical landmark, street corner, or lamp pole number to guide night crews straight to the spot.

Resident Safety First
