From resident signal to accountable action

Civic participation should never feel like tossing words into a dark void. SANJAYA structures the journey from citizen notification to physical remediation with dignity and mutual clarity.

The System Journey

How a report travels through the municipal machine

01

Intake & De-duplication

When an issue is filed, SANJAYA immediately scans a 35-meter radius for existing reports under the same category. If neighbors have already signaled the same defect, the reports are intelligently clustered into one master action order.

Benefit: Municipal staff are not bombarded with five different calls for one road pit; resources stay focused on repairs.
02

Coordination & Priority Review

A duty coordinator checks whether the issue borders a metro line, tram track, or state highway to prevent jurisdictional passing-the-buck. Priority scores factor in road classification, pedestrian traffic density, and weather risk.

Benefit: Critical hazards near schools and hospitals receive expedited rapid-response mobilization.
03

Field Dispatch & Work In Progress

The task moves to the field crew's daily dispatch schedule. Heavy civil works (like asphalt resurfacing) are typically batched for nocturnal shifts (22:00 to 05:00) to avoid paralyzing daylight public transit.

Benefit: Residents can see the scheduled work shift on their ticket timeline.
04

Inspection, Photo Verification & Archival

A case cannot be marked 'Resolved' through paperwork alone. Field supervisors log a site inspection with photographic verification before the ticket is formally closed and archived into the public transparency record.

Benefit: Accountable sign-off that builds neighborhood trust.
Editorial Insight

Why a clear report helps a responsible office act faster

Municipal workers frequently express that vague reports ("the road near the market is broken") require half a day of detective work just to locate the site. When a resident provides a photo with a distinctive background building, landmark, and exact ward, the duty officer can assign materials and schedule a crew in under ten minutes.
