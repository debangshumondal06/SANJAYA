Public Purpose & Principles

A civic tool built around clarity and respect

SANJAYA was conceived to close the distance between citizen observation and municipal remediation. We believe that public infrastructure redresses should inspire trust rather than fatigue.

The SANJAYA Product Thesis
"SANJAYA should make civic participation feel clear, dignified, and consequential. The interface should help a resident understand what to do next within seconds, while making the system's values—evidence, transparency, accountability, and respect—visible in every screen."
— Product Requirements Document, September 2026
Foundational Principles

How SANJAYA is designed to behave

1. Civic Dignity

Use calm language, generous space, and clear ownership. Never make a resident feel like an anonymous ticket number in a cold bureaucratic queue.

2. Public Before Operational

The default portal helps residents act, report, and understand. Complex internal dispatch dashboards remain visually and navigationally separated.

3. Classical, Not Nostalgic

Use proportion, heritage maroon tones, antique gold accents, and typographic texture to evoke institutional gravitas without faux-official seals or clutter.

4. Visible Progress

Always show what happens next, what has happened so far, and what the resident can do now. Eliminate the anxiety of dead-end submission screens.

5. Evidence with Empathy

Request only necessary information and explain clearly why it helps municipal crews. Support photo, text, location, and multilingual voice recordings.

6. Quiet Confidence

Use motion and polish to reinforce hierarchy, not to compete with the civic task. Avoid distracting animated confetti or flashing counters.

Inclusivity Commitment

Accessibility Posture (WCAG 2.2 AA)

Civic infrastructure belongs to everyone. SANJAYA has been engineered from the ground up to support residents of diverse physical abilities, devices, and digital confidence:

  • High Contrast Palette: Parchment text (`#2D2545`) on deep maroon surfaces (`#FFFFFF` & `#EAF2F8`) exceeds AA contrast ratios.
  • Dual Information Coding: Statuses are never conveyed by color alone; every state is paired with clear text and distinctive iconography.
  • Keyboard & Focus Navigation: Interactive elements feature a visible double-ring outline (`#2F6FA3`) and complete Tab key traversability.
  • Touch Target Sizing: All buttons and interactive hit-targets meet or exceed the 44 × 44 pixel mobile standard.
  • Screen Reader Ready: Explicit ARIA live regions for assistant replies and ticket search updates.
Resident Privacy

Privacy by Design

Reporting a dangerous pothole or overflowing trash vat should not require surrendering personal biographical data.

  • No Compulsory Login: Citizens can report issues and track tickets without creating accounts.
  • Optional Contact Details: Name and phone number fields are completely voluntary and only used if a road engineer needs on-site clarification.
  • No Tracking Cookies: The public portal does not utilize commercial advertising cookies or user profiling trackers.
